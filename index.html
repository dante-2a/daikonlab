How this works

  • Each day gets its own log automatically. Old days stay in the list on the left.
  • Conversations are separate rooms anyone can create, join, and post in.
  • Going offline (closing the tab, switching away, or signing off) marks where you left off — a red line shows what you missed when you're back.
  • You can delete your own messages. You can delete a conversation you created.

Admin controls

Passwords are hashed and can't be viewed, even by ADMIN — you can remove an account instead.
